LEARNED COMPUTING LANGUAGE (LCL)
TITLE: Declaration Resolution, Reachability, and Execution Graph
LANGUAGE VERSION: 0.1.0
STATUS: NORMATIVE
DATE: 2026-08-30


Definitions and source structure are immutable during one execution. Forward
references are valid after full-document resolution; unresolved or cyclic
prohibited references invalidate before effects.

EXECUTE identifies one root. Reachability follows TASK/PHASE/SEQUENCE/STEP/
ACTION references, TRUE branches, and applicable handlers. Unreachable ACTION
and ALLOW declarations do not execute. Reachability does not expand SCOPE or
permission. Each declaration evaluates at most once except bounded loop runtime
instances and explicit RETRY attempts.
