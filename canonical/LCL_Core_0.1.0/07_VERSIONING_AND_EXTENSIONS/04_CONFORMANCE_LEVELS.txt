LEARNED COMPUTING LANGUAGE (LCL)
TITLE: Interpreter Conformance Levels
LANGUAGE VERSION: 0.1.0
STATUS: NORMATIVE
DATE: 2026-08-30


VALIDATOR
    Decodes, parses, resolves, type-checks, and reports exact errors.

EVALUATOR
    VALIDATOR plus pure expressions, scope/rule/conflict/dependency/check/status
    evaluation without external effects.

EXECUTOR
    EVALUATOR plus core operations supported by host, action authorization,
    evidence, and declared outputs.

PORTABLE LC CONFORMANT
    Passes required cross-model test protocol for claimed level/version and does
    not use hidden ordinary-language requirements.

A host may report unsupported operations but cannot claim executor conformance
for them.
