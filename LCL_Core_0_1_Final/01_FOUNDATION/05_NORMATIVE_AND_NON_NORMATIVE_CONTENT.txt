LEARNED COMPUTING LANGUAGE (LCL)
TITLE: Normative and Non-Normative Content
LANGUAGE VERSION: 0.1.0
STATUS: NORMATIVE
DATE: 2026-08-30


Normative content consists of grammar-recognized blocks/fields, exact imported
rules, active extension definitions, typed values, and expressions.

DESCRIPTION, COMMENT, and ordinary prose inside strings are non-normative unless
a normative field explicitly treats the string as data/meaning. EXAMPLE is
non-normative unless an explicit TEST evaluates it. A requirement hidden only
in prose is absent.
