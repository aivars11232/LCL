LEARNED COMPUTING LANGUAGE (LCL)
TITLE: Design Principles
LANGUAGE VERSION: 0.2.0
STATUS: NORMATIVE
DATE: 2026-08-30


1. Explicit source controls; conversational context does not.
2. Technical English remains readable but has constrained syntax. Reserved
   words may also be spelled through a validated locale profile; the canonical
   token identities and meaning do not change.
3. One keyword has one core meaning.
4. Exact identifiers replace vague references.
5. Hard rules and soft preferences are distinct.
6. Permission is distinct from requirement.
7. Missing, unknown, and null are distinct.
8. Every repetition and retry is bounded.
9. Side effects, scope, and success are observable.
10. Domain vocabulary extends the core without changing core grammar.
11. Unsupported syntax fails closed.
12. Equal valid input under deterministic declarations has equivalent meaning
    across conforming interpreters.
