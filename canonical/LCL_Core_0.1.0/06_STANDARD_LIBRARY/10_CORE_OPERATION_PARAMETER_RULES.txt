LCL CORE 0.1.0 RELEASE CANDIDATE
CORE OPERATION CONTRACT RULES
NORMATIVE

Every core operation's candidate target, named-parameter, precondition,
postcondition, error, side-effect, determinism, and result-schema records are
stored in 10_REGISTRIES/operations_v0.1.0.json. Named-parameter membership and
required/default status are closed. The candidate is not contract-complete:
25 operations have no determinism declaration, result schemas do not declare
field cardinality/openness, and result-to-OUTPUT binding is unresolved.

No positional parameters exist. A parameter absent from an operation contract is
forbidden. A required parameter cannot be inferred. A default applies only to a
MISSING optional parameter. An operation may cause only its listed side effects
and effects explicitly required by its postconditions. Additional effects produce
error.permission.denied or error.scope.violation.
