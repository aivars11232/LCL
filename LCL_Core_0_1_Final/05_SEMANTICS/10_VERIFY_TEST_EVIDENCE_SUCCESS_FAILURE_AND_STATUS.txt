LEARNED COMPUTING LANGUAGE (LCL)
TITLE: Verify, Test, Evidence, Success, Failure, and Status
LANGUAGE VERSION: 0.1.0
STATUS: NORMATIVE
DATE: 2026-08-30


VALIDATE observes preconditions. VERIFY observes postconditions. TEST compares
EXPECTED/ACTUAL or ASSERT. Required failed checks prevent success. EVIDENCE must
be observable, typed, and traceable; declared required provenance/checksum must
resolve.

SUCCESS evaluates ALL, ANY, or NONE after execution/checks. status.succeeded is
legal only when SUCCESS is TRUE, all required outputs exist, all hard rules hold,
and all required evidence exists. status.partial is explicitly non-success.
FAILURE blocks may map exact WHEN conditions to terminal non-success statuses.
