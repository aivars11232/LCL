LEARNED COMPUTING LANGUAGE (LCL)
TITLE: Extension Contract
LANGUAGE VERSION: 0.1.0
STATUS: NORMATIVE
DATE: 2026-08-30


An extension is kind.extension and may contain IMPORT, DEFINE, DATA, COMMENT,
and EXAMPLE only. It may define lowercase namespaced terms, types, operations,
formats, events, statuses, errors, and units. It cannot add uppercase reserved
keywords, alter grammar/precedence/core meanings, add hidden rules/tasks, replace
built-ins, or execute. Domain operations require exact parameter/result/effect/
determinism definitions.
