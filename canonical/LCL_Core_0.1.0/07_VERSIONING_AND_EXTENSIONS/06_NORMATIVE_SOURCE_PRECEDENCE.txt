The global authority order is defined only by
00_RELEASE/02_NORMATIVE_AUTHORITY_ORDER.txt. This document does not reorder its
tiers.

Within one tier, the entry that governs the exact subject is more specific than a
summary entry in that same artifact class. For example, an operation's exact
contract governs that operation within the registry tier, and an exact block
signature governs that block within the block-signature registry. Specificity
never permits a lower global tier to override a higher one.

Two incompatible exact entries at the same global tier are a specification defect
to be repaired in a patch. An interpreter must report the contradiction rather
than invent behavior. Examples and research basis are informative unless
explicitly marked normative.
