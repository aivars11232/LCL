LEARNED COMPUTING LANGUAGE (LCL)
TITLE: Task, Action, Phase, Sequence, and Step Form
LANGUAGE VERSION: 0.1.0
STATUS: NORMATIVE
DATE: 2026-08-30


TASK references its GOAL and SUCCESS plus one or more PHASE, SEQUENCE, or ACTION
declarations. Those TASK fields accept references/lists only and do not open
inline child blocks. ACTION names one OPERATION and exact target/parameters/output.
SEQUENCE orders STEP lexically. PHASE groups steps and may contain nested SEQUENCE
blocks under its declared legal-parent contract; it may also add BEFORE/AFTER.

A STEP selects exactly one execution structure. ACTION accepts a reference/list
or one inline nested ACTION. SEQUENCE and PHASE accept references/lists only;
TASK accepts one exact reference. mode.parallel is legal only when all included
actions have proven independence, including no conflicting writes, read/write
dependencies, shared mutable bindings, or conflicting output destinations.
Disjoint output paths alone are insufficient; otherwise validation fails.
The execution_graph_contract in block_schemas_v0.1.0.json defines exact child
order, activation identity, dependency edges, loop instances, and successors.
An ACTION selects at most one OUTPUT reference; TASK and EXECUTE may list output
requirements or exports under the separate output-projection contract.
