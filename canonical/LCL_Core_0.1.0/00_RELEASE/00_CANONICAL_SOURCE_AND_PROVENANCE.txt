4. VERSION AND STATUS

The historical artifacts are candidate builds; the workspace contains no evidence
that either materially different 0.1.0 candidate was an established release. This
repair therefore retains language version 0.1.0. Accepted Tasks 0001 through 0004
resolve LCL-AUDIT-001, 005, LCL-AUDIT-010/K-003, 011, 012, and the
operation-contract portion of LCL-AUDIT-013 through the LIST/SET/ITEM,
constructor/pattern, field value-kind/parent closure, division/SET sorting,
PRIORITY, and 39-row operation contracts. This candidate remains blocked by the
bare-language result-binding portion of LCL-AUDIT-007, the result-schema
cardinality/output portion of LCL-AUDIT-013, and findings LCL-AUDIT-014 and 015.
Parser/runtime/executable-semantic evidence under LCL-AUDIT-007 and LCL-AUDIT-016
is outside the bare-language package scope and is not a release blocker. The
candidate is not ready for release, no repair archive is produced, and it must
not be represented as Final, Verified, Complete, Canonical Release, or
release-ready.
