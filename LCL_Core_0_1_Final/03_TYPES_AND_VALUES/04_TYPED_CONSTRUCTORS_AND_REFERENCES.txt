    REF(identifier)
    PATH("/absolute/path")
    PATH(REF(workspace.id), "relative/path")
    URI("https://example.invalid/resource")
    GLOB("src/**/*.py")
    REGEX("^[a-z]+$")
    DATE("2026-08-30")
    TIME("14:30:00+02:00")
    DATETIME("2026-08-30T14:30:00+02:00")
    DURATION(5, unit.second)
    PERCENTAGE(90)
    BYTES(1024)
    MEASURE(1920, unit.pixel)

PATH with one relative string is legal only as IMPORT/EXTENSION SOURCE, resolved
relative to the importing document. Other relative paths require WORKSPACE.
REF accepts an identifier, not a quoted string, and resolves exactly once.
