LEARNED COMPUTING LANGUAGE (LCL)
TITLE: Task, Action, Phase, Sequence, and Step Form
LANGUAGE VERSION: 0.1.0
STATUS: NORMATIVE
DATE: 2026-08-30


TASK references its GOAL and SUCCESS plus one or more execution structures.
ACTION names one OPERATION and exact target/parameters/output. SEQUENCE orders
STEP lexically. PHASE groups steps/sequences and may add BEFORE/AFTER.

A STEP contains exactly one ACTION, SEQUENCE, PHASE, or TASK reference/nested
ACTION. mode.parallel is legal only when all included actions have disjoint side
effects or explicit conflict-free output targets; otherwise validation fails.
