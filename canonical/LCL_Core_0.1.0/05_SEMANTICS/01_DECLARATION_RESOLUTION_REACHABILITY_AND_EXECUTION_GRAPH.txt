LEARNED COMPUTING LANGUAGE (LCL)
TITLE: Declaration Resolution, Reachability, and Execution Graph
LANGUAGE VERSION: 0.1.0
STATUS: NORMATIVE
DATE: 2026-08-30


Definitions and source structure are immutable during one execution. Forward
references are valid after full-document resolution; unresolved or cyclic
prohibited references invalidate before effects.

Resolution uses the complete lexical namespace environment of each declaring
document. Imported full IDs receive the exact importing prefix; namespace
ownership and collision rules are in
02_LEXICAL/03_IDENTIFIERS_NAMESPACES_AND_RESERVED_NAMES.txt. Forward resolution
does not evaluate a referenced value or start a referenced execution unit.

Static checking establishes identity, type, legal fields, and dependency order.
Value-dependent expressions are evaluated only at their declared point of
reachability or demand. Assertions concerning OUTPUT are not evaluated before
the producing actions merely because their references resolve statically.
REF reads follow types_v0.1.0.json#/reference_context_contract. An ordinary read
of a not-yet-bound OUTPUT yields MISSING; it never starts that OUTPUT's producer.
A metadata read such as REF(output.copy).TARGET reads the declaration field
without requiring the OUTPUT's result binding.

VALIDATE and VERIFY expose the Boolean result of their declared check in value
context. SUCCESS expressions therefore consume their check results rather than
their reference identities. Resolving such a REF does not rerun the check or
execute an operation. The check's registered handling of MISSING, UNKNOWN, and
diagnostics applies before a required Boolean result can establish success.

EXECUTE identifies one root. Reachability follows TASK/PHASE/SEQUENCE/STEP/
ACTION references, TRUE branches, and applicable handlers. Unreachable ACTION
and ALLOW declarations do not execute. Reachability does not expand SCOPE or
permission. Each declaration evaluates at most once per declared evaluation
point except bounded loop instances and explicit RETRY attempts. Distinct loop
instances have distinct local binding identities; source declaration identity
and imported namespace ownership remain fixed.
