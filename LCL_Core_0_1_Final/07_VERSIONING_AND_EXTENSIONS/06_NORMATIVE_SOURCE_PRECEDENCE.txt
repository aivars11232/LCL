For accidental wording overlap, specificity order is:

1. exact machine-readable registry entry;
2. exact block schema;
3. exact standard-library entry;
4. EBNF plus grammar semantic constraints;
5. subject-specific semantic rule;
6. lexical rule;
7. foundation overview.

A genuine contradiction is a specification defect to be repaired in a patch;
an interpreter must report it rather than invent behavior. Examples and research
basis are informative unless explicitly marked normative.
